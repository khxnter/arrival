Lavendar Bushes

🌱 1. Prune twice a year (this is the big one)

After flowering (mid–late summer):
Cut back the flower stems and about ⅓ of the green growth

Early spring (after last frost):
Light trim to shape and encourage new shoots

This stops it stretching out and going woody.


✂️ 2. Never cut into the woody part

The thick, brown, woody stems won’t regrow properly
Always leave some soft green growth on every stem

👉 Rule of thumb:

If it’s flexible and green = safe to cut
If it’s hard and woody = don’t touch it


🌿 3. Shape it like a dome

Keep it rounded, not flat on top
This helps light reach all parts → encourages even, bushy growth


🔁 4. Don’t skip years

If you leave it unpruned:

  • It gets leggy
  • Splits in the middle
  • Goes woody fast

Once it’s woody, you can’t really reverse it, only replace it.


🌞 5. Give it the right conditions

Lavender stays tighter when it’s slightly stressed:

  • Full sun
  • Well-drained soil (important in NL with rain)
  • Don’t overwater or overfeed
