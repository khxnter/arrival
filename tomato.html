Tomato Growing guide

Seeds:

  • Start growing late feb / mid march
  • Grow 18-24 inches apart
  • Make sure it has good drainage holes
  • Use crushed egg shells for calcium
  • Use fertiliser
  • When they get the second pair of leaves move to their own small pots
  • Don’t plant in seed trays, not deep enough soil, plant seeds in individual pots

Outdoors:

  • Last frost End of May
  • Put into big pots end of May
  • Use scissors to prune off the lower leaves
  • Watch out for sun damage
  • Cover soil surface with mulch, prevents blossom end rot. Keeps regular moisture in the soil so it doesnt go from wet to dry so quickly.

Later on:

After about 1m tall cut off the bottom leaf branches.

If leggy:

Bury stem up to first set of leaves.

Tomato sauce:

  • Slice garlic
  • Basil
  • Onion
  • Olive oil
  • Salt pepper
