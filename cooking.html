Chocolate Chip Flapjacks

  • 100g Oats
  • 1/4 block of butter (50g)
  • lots of honey (50g)
  • Nearly a pack of chocolate chips (40g)
  • Same with mixed seeds

Heat up and mix butter and honey, mix into oats, leave to cool slightly.

Mix in seeds with the oats.

Add chocolate chips, mix a bit, don't let them melt too much.

Make very flat and put into baking paper, on rack in airfryer.

160c for 15 mins?
