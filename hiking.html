Hiking in the Highlands

  • Hiking Gear doc

Things to remember:

  • Backpack 12kg max
  • Don't take too many spare clothes, like 3 of everything for a week.
  • Everything quick drying (Bamboo) - socks/boxers/t-shirts
  • Take spare shoes - like flip flops
  • £200 - £300 on boots
  • Head torch very important
  • Get a head net for midges!

Equipment:

  • Under 1kg sleeping bag, comfort for 5c (extreme -15c)
  • Sleeping bag inner fleece
  • Sleeping bag blow-up mat
