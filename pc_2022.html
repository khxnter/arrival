Power Saving Notes

  • Turn off GPU Tweak III
  • Change CPU to variable clock speed in the BIOS (I have two presets saved)
  • Change power mode in NVidea 3D settings from Max Perf -> Normal

CPU goes from static 4.6 to around 3.7 and drops 3c.
